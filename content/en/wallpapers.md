Pick a scene in its light or dark version. Download it and set it as
your desktop background.

Each wallpaper is 5120 by 2880 pixels (16:9), a JPEG of 1 to 3 MB. Any
screen of that shape scales it down. On a phone, choose the part you want when you set it.
The meshcore-info.eu wordmark sits in the bottom right corner.

## Using them {#use}

Use the wallpapers as the background of your own screens, and share them as
they are: the wordmark in the corner names the source. Do not
use them in ads, on products or merchandise. The
[Promo & press kit](/press-kit/) has the logo, more images and a short text
about the site.
