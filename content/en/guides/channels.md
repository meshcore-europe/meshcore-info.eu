A channel is a group chat on the mesh. A channel whose name starts with #
takes its key from that name. In the MeshCore app, add a hashtag channel and
type its name, with the #. The Public channel is already set up.

The list below comes from the [MeshCore Analyzer](https://meshcore-analyzer.eu/).
It decodes the channels its observers hear across Europe. An observer is a node
that forwards what it hears over MQTT. Only channels whose key anyone can know
are listed: the #channels and Public. The page shows how active a channel is,
not its messages.
