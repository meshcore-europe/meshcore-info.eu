Pick a board here, then [Get started](/get-started/) flashes it and pairs the
app. For your first node, pick a **companion** with a built-in battery. You carry
it and message from the app on your phone or laptop, over Bluetooth or USB.
Some have their own screen and keyboard and work on their own. For a
**repeater** (a node that passes messages on, mostly on solar power), pick a
low-power board with the nRF52840 processor. A **room server** stores
messages so people can read them later. Get the 868 MHz version, and attach
the antenna before you switch it on. The [antenna guide](/guides/antenna/)
covers which antenna, connector and cable a node needs.

Every board below has an official MeshCore build for each role it shows.
Each card links the makers' specifications and the date we last checked them.
We link to no shop. The price bands come from the makers' list prices where
they publish one, checked October 2026: € is under 25 euros, €€ 25 to 60,
€€€ above 60.

Your board is not listed? The [official flasher](https://flasher.meshcore.io)
lists every board MeshCore supports.
