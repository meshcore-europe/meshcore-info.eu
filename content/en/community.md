Your local group knows which radio settings your area uses, where the
repeaters (the nodes that pass messages on) are and which channels are
active. Ask them your questions, in
your own language. Questions that cross borders? Ask on our
[Discord](https://discord.meshcore-info.eu/).

Every group here is public, and we check each link before we add it. Each
group runs its own chats and sites; this page links to them.
