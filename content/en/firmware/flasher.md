Pick your board and its role. A companion is the node you message from, over
Bluetooth or USB. A repeater passes messages on. A room server keeps messages
for a group. The newest MeshCore release is chosen for
you. The firmware comes from MeshCore's own releases on GitHub; this site
passes the file on and changes nothing.
