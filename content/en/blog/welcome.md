meshcore-info.eu explains MeshCore for Europe: the settings, guides and
tools the local meshes share. The press release below is free to copy.

## Press release {#press-release}

Copy this text for your news site, newsletter or group chat. Use it as it
is, or shorten it. Name meshcore-info.eu as the source, in lowercase, with .eu.

**meshcore-info.eu brings MeshCore settings, guides and tools together for Europe.**

**Who and what.** MeshCore is a way to message off the grid. It
sends short texts by radio, node to node, without the internet or a
subscription. Repeaters, small LoRa radios, pass each message on. The mesh
keeps working in a power cut or when the internet is down, as long as the
repeaters have power. It
carries no voice or photos, and does not replace the emergency services. On
22 October 2026, meshcore-analyzer.eu had heard 30,240 repeaters in
Europe in 7 days.

Local groups run those repeaters. Until now, their settings,
guides and tools sat per country or per community. meshcore-info.eu brings
them together for Europe, in 16 languages. Volunteers write it, without ads,
shop links or tracking.

**How it works.**

- Open meshcore-info.eu in your language: the site speaks 16.
- Find guides on [hardware](/devices/), roles and [settings](/guides/), and on
  [how MeshCore works](/about/).
- Find the [local group](/community/) near you, or add yours.
- Add the settings your area uses to [Recommended settings](/guides/settings/).
- Correct anything on the site, like a wiki. A volunteer checks each change
  before it shows on the site. [Contribute](/contribute/) shows how, with or
  without a GitHub account.

**Why it matters.** Communities across Europe run MeshCore side by side. With
the right settings, their meshes form one mesh.

## The promises {#promises}

- **No ads, shop links or affiliate links.** The [Devices](/devices/) page
  lists the makers' specifications and a price band. It never links to a
  shop.
- **No tracking.** The site runs no analytics or trackers. The only cookie remembers your
  theme choice. The only other server the site asks is meshcore-analyzer.eu,
  run by the same volunteers. The [privacy page](/privacy/) has the details.
- **Sources for facts.** What we write about MeshCore comes from its firmware
  and documentation. Radio rules come from the regulators.
- **Your language.** The site is available in 16 languages. Pages start as
  machine translations, marked as such until a native speaker has checked
  them.

Everything is open source. Your local group missing, or a fact wrong?
