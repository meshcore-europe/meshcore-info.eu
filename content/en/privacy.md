## What this site does not do {#not}

- It runs no analytics, statistics or tracking scripts, ours or anyone else's.
- It loads its fonts, styles and scripts from this site only. There are no
  embedded videos from other sites, no social media buttons and no ads.
- It has no accounts or comments. The two forms on the site, the key
  generator and the channel picker, send nothing to us.

## What stays in your browser {#browser}

When you pick follow system, dark or light in the theme menu, your browser
keeps that choice in a cookie called `dmc-mode` for 1 year. It is set on
meshcore-info.eu and its subdomains, so every page opens the way you chose.
The cookie holds only that word. If you never pick, the site sets no cookie.
Clear your browser's site data to remove it.

The home also remembers for this tab that you saw its opening film, so a
second visit in the same tab opens at the content. That note is gone when you
close the tab.

On a page with a checklist, such as the
[deployment checklist](/repeater-guide/#checklist), your browser keeps which
items you ticked, so they are still ticked when you come back. They stay on
your device until you untick them.

The front page reads your browser's preferred languages and opens the
matching language version of the site. That check runs in your browser and
sends nothing. Once it has picked a language, or you pick one in the
language menu, your browser keeps that language on your device. The front
page then opens in it next time, without the note about the language.
Clear your browser's site data to remove it.

## What the site asks other servers {#requests}

The home and the Network page show live numbers from
[meshcore-analyzer.eu](https://meshcore-analyzer.eu), run by the same group of
volunteers. The Find channels page asks that server for the channels of the
country you pick. Each request carries your IP address, your browser's name
and version (the user agent) and, on Find channels, the country you chose. No
other page contacts another server.

## What our host sees {#host}

Cloudflare, a hosting company, sends you every page. To send you a page,
Cloudflare's servers handle your request. That request includes your IP
address, the address of the page and your browser's user agent, as with any
web server. Cloudflare's [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
describes how it handles that data. Cloudflare also asks your browser to
report a failed connection to it, so it can see outages.

The volunteers who run this site do not switch on Cloudflare's web analytics
and have no access to logs of individual visitors.

## Links to other sites {#links}

Links on this site go to local groups, maps, projects, manufacturers and
GitHub. Once you follow one, that site's own privacy policy applies. The
"Edit this page on GitHub" links take you to GitHub.
