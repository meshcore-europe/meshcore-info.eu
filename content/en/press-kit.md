## About the site {#about}

**In 1 sentence.** meshcore-info.eu explains MeshCore for Europe: the
settings, guides and tools the local meshes share.

**In 1 paragraph.** MeshCore sends messages by radio, without the internet
or a subscription. Small LoRa radios called repeaters pass each message on,
and across Europe their meshes connect into one. Regions, the areas a message
stays in, keep traffic local, so your settings depend on where you live. meshcore-info.eu collects those settings, the
guides and the tools in one place, in 16 languages. It also points you to
the local group near you. Volunteers write it, without ads, shop links or
tracking.

Copy either text as it is, or shorten it. Name the site as meshcore-info.eu,
in lowercase, with .eu. For a longer text, copy the press release in the
[launch post](/blog/welcome/).

## Using it {#use}

Use the logo, icon, images and videos in news, posts and talks about
meshcore-info.eu or MeshCore. Use them to link to the site too. Name
meshcore-info.eu as the source, with a link where you can. You may scale and
crop them, and shorten the videos. Use the wallpapers as the background of
your own screens.

Do not use them in ads, on products or merchandise, or in a way that
suggests we endorse you. The music in the launch video belongs to its
makers: use it only with this video.

The texts on this page and the rest of the site are free to use under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

meshcore-info.eu is a separate site about the mesh in Europe. The MeshCore
developers build the firmware and the app, at [meshcore.io](https://meshcore.io). For
the MeshCore logo and texts, go there.
